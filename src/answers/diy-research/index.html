DIY research with aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: DIY research on aytm gives you full control of survey design, targeting, and analysis while still offering guided solutions and resources when you need them.

Overview

In a DIY model, your team uses the aytm platform directly to create surveys, define audiences, and review results. The interface is built for researchers and non-researchers alike, with tools such as logic, quotas, conversation ai, and visual reporting. You can start from a blank survey, duplicate past work, or use xpert solutions as templates to move faster while staying aligned with best practices.

Key points

  • DIY users control survey authoring, sample setup, and analysis in the platform.
  • aytm provides xpert solutions, academy content, and help center documentation to support best practices.
  • You can add full-service support for select projects without changing your DIY workflows.

FAQs

Who is DIY research best suited for?

DIY research is a good fit for teams that want speed and control, and that have at least some internal comfort with survey design, targeting, and interpreting results.

What support is available for DIY users?

DIY users can access aytm academy, the help center, and xpert solutions, and may also work with aytm representatives for onboarding or occasional guidance.

Can I move between DIY and full-service?

Yes. You can run some projects DIY and bring in full-service support for others, using the same core platform and data environment across both models.
