How fast will my survey complete on aytm?

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: Most surveys on aytm complete within hours, but actual timing depends on incidence rate, targeting complexity, and sample size, which the platform estimates before launch.

Overview

Completion time for a survey on aytm is shaped by how easy it is to find qualified respondents and how many completes you need. Broader, high-incidence audiences typically finish more quickly than narrow or heavily screened populations. The platform’s pricing and delivery tool estimates completion windows based on your audience, length of interview, and sample size, so you can see expected timing before you launch.

Key points

  • High-incidence, broadly targeted surveys often complete in a matter of hours.
  • Low-incidence, niche audiences or complex quota structures can extend timelines.
  • aytm’s delivery estimate updates in real time as you adjust targeting and completes.

FAQs

What factors affect how fast my survey completes?

Key factors include incidence rate, targeting complexity, geographic scope, and the total number of completes you order. Shorter, simpler surveys aimed at broad groups typically finish faster.

Where do I see the estimated completion time?

The pricing and delivery section of the aytm interface displays an estimated completion window, which updates as you modify your targeting, sample size, and length of interview.

Can I speed up a slow survey?

In some cases, you can speed up completion by loosening targeting criteria, adjusting quotas, or increasing the incidence rate assumptions after consulting with the aytm team.
