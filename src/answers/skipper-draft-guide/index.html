Skipper AI draft guide: how to create strong first drafts

Last updated: 2025-11-14

TL;DR: Skipper AI helps you quickly turn research objectives into survey drafts. Start with a clear prompt, refine wording, add logic, customize sections, and validate with a preview. Skipper makes the drafting process faster and more consistent across teams.

What is the Skipper draft workflow?

Skipper AI turns your research goals into structured survey drafts. You provide the objectives and target audience, and Skipper generates questions, logic, and structure to get you started.

How to create a strong survey draft with Skipper

1. Start with a clear prompt

Your Skipper prompt should include:

  • Research goals
  • Target audience
  • Key decisions the study needs to inform
  • Any required modules (concepts, pricing, satisfaction, etc.)

2. Generate the first draft

Skipper will produce:

  • An outline
  • Draft questions
  • Suggested scales
  • Optional screener items

3. Refine question wording

  • Remove jargon
  • Simplify long questions
  • Ensure neutrality
  • Split double-barreled items

4. Add logic and structure

  • Skip logic
  • Piping
  • Randomization
  • Branching for different user types

5. Customize modules

Replace Skipper placeholders with your actual concepts, messages, or features.

6. Validate with a soft launch

  • Check drop-off patterns
  • Review open-ends for confusion
  • Ensure logic routes correctly

Tips for writing strong Skipper prompts

  • Include examples of the tone or style you want
  • Specify question types (grids, MaxDiff, open-ends, etc.)
  • Call out “must-have” items or modules
  • Define the expected survey length

FAQs

Can Skipper generate logic automatically?

Yes—Skipper suggests logic, but always review and adjust for your specific study.

What if my objectives change?

Update your prompt and regenerate a new section or full draft—Skipper adapts quickly.

Can Skipper handle multi-cell or monadic designs?

Yes. Skipper can draft monadic blocks and cell-level variations once you specify the design.
