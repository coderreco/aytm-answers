aytm solutions center

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: The aytm solutions center is a catalog of guided research solutions that help you choose and configure study designs for product, brand, experience, and strategic questions.

Overview

The solutions center organizes aytm’s research solutions into an accessible library. You can browse by business objective or lifecycle stage and see which approaches are recommended for product development, brand tracking, campaign evaluation, customer experience, and broader business strategy. Each solution is built on best-practice methods and can be adapted to your needs.

Key points

  • Solutions are grouped by research objective, such as product, brand, experience, marketing, and strategy.
  • Each listing describes the methodology, sample considerations, and typical use cases.
  • You can use the solutions center to pick a starting point and then tailor details in the aytm platform.

FAQs

How do I use the solutions center?

Start by selecting a category aligned with your business question—for example, product development or brand insights—then review the available solutions to find the one that best matches your objectives and timeline.

Can I customize solutions from the center?

Yes. Solutions are starting points. Once selected, you can adjust survey content, targeting, and quotas in the aytm platform to fit your specific needs.

Are solutions only for full-service projects?

No. Many solutions are designed for self-serve use or light guidance, though you can also combine them with full-service support if desired.
