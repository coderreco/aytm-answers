Market landscape research with aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: Market landscape research on aytm helps you map categories, competitors, and consumer segments so you can spot opportunities and sharpen strategy.

Overview

aytm’s market landscape solutions help teams understand how buyers see a category, which players they recognize, and how needs differ across segments. Using guided designs and flexible question types, you can explore brand awareness, usage patterns, consideration sets, and whitespace. Insights from these studies often feed into product roadmaps, positioning, and go-to-market plans.

Key points

  • Use aytm to quantify brand awareness, consideration, and usage within a category.
  • Explore segments by demographics, behaviors, or attitudes to see how needs vary.
  • Outputs can inform strategy, positioning, and prioritization of future initiatives.

FAQs

What questions can market landscape research answer?

It can clarify who the main players are, how consumers perceive them, what needs are met or unmet, and where there may be opportunity spaces for your brand or product.

How detailed can segmentation be?

You can segment by demographics, behaviors, or attitudes, depending on your sample and survey design, and use aytm’s reporting tools to compare groups side by side.

When should I run a landscape study?

Teams often run landscape research before major strategy decisions, category entries, or repositioning efforts, and then refresh it periodically as markets evolve.
