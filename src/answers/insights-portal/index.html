Insights portal on aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: The aytm insights portal is a centralized hub where teams can find, access, and reuse research results and dashboards.

Overview

The insights portal brings research outputs into one place, making it easier for teams to find past studies, revisit dashboards, and share findings with stakeholders. Instead of searching across scattered files and decks, users can browse or search within the portal and open relevant projects directly in the aytm environment, keeping data connected to its source.

Key points

  • Centralizes access to study results and dashboards in one location.
  • Helps teams avoid duplicating work by making past research easier to find.
  • Supports ongoing knowledge sharing across insights, marketing, product, and leadership teams.

FAQs

Who can access the insights portal?

Access depends on your organization’s setup and permissions, but it is typically available to insights, marketing, product, and other stakeholders who use research.

How does the portal differ from individual dashboards?

Dashboards show results for a single study, while the portal is a higher-level entry point that helps you find and open those dashboards and projects across your portfolio.

Can the portal support search across projects?

The portal is designed to make it easier to locate relevant work across multiple studies, reducing the time spent searching for past research before planning new work.
