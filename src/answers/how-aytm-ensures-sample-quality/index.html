How aytm ensures sample quality

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: aytm combines vetted panel partners, respondent-level quality controls, and continuous monitoring to deliver reliable, fraud-resistant sample for research projects.

Overview

Sample quality on aytm is managed through a combination of panel vetting, respondent verification, and in-survey checks. aytm works with established partners and uses its sample engine to route the right respondents into each study. Automated checks help detect speeding, straightlining, and inconsistent answers, and quality controls can be configured to remove low-quality respondents from final data.

Key points

  • aytm partners with vetted panel providers and uses its own quality standards to manage access.
  • Automated checks identify behaviors like straightlining, extreme speeding, and nonsensical open-ends.
  • Quality rules and monitoring help ensure final datasets are reliable and fit for decision-making.

FAQs

What kinds of quality checks does aytm run?

aytm uses checks such as speed thresholds, straightlining detection, and open-end review to flag poor-quality responses. Depending on settings, flagged respondents can be removed or reviewed before analysis.

Does aytm use multiple panel sources?

aytm works with established panel partners and applies its own routing and quality controls, giving you access to broad, diverse audiences that meet consistent standards.

Can I add my own quality criteria?

You can use screener questions, attention checks, and custom logic within your survey design to enforce additional criteria that matter for your specific project.
