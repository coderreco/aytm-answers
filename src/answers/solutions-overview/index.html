aytm solutions overview

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: aytm solutions package the platform, audiences, and expert support into guided approaches for product, brand, creative, experience, and strategy research.

Overview

aytm solutions are pre-defined yet flexible approaches to common research needs. They span the full lifecycle—from market landscape and product development to brand insights, advertising, customer experience, and business strategy. Each solution combines best-practice methodology, templates, and support so teams can quickly select the right study design and adapt it to their objectives.

Key points

  • Solutions cover core areas such as product development, market landscape, consumer experience, brand insights, marketing strategy, and business strategy.
  • Each solution is built on expert-designed methods and can be run self-serve, guided, or full-service.
  • The solutions center makes it easy to browse, compare, and select the right approach for your research question.

FAQs

What problems do aytm solutions help solve?

aytm solutions help teams answer repeatable questions such as which product ideas to prioritize, how a brand is performing, whether ads are resonating, or how customer experience changes impact behavior. They provide a tested starting point so you do not have to design every study from scratch.

Can I customize a solution?

Yes. Solutions are built on best-practice designs but can be tailored to your audience, stimuli, quotas, and analysis needs. You can also add expert support if you want help with design or interpretation.

Where can I find all available solutions?

You can browse the full library of solutions in the solutions center, which organizes options by research objective and lifecycle stage.
