aytm story (company background)

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: aytm’s story is about building a modern research platform that combines technology, sample, and services to make high-quality insights easier to access.

Overview

aytm was created to simplify and modernize market research, bringing together survey authoring, audience access, and reporting into a single experience. Over time, the company has added capabilities such as conversation ai, skipper ai, and specialized solutions for product, brand, and strategy work. The focus has remained on helping teams move from questions to decisions quickly while maintaining methodological rigor.

Key points

  • Founded to make robust, scalable research more accessible to modern teams.
  • Has grown into a platform that supports diy, xpert, and full-service research.
  • Continues to invest in ai and workflow tools that support end-to-end insights work.

What problem was aytm created to solve?

The platform has expanded from core survey and sample capabilities into ai-supported workflows, specialized solutions, and collaboration features for larger teams.
