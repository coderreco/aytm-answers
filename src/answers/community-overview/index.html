aytm community overview

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: The aytm community is the respondent ecosystem that powers studies, combining proprietary communities and panel partners under shared quality standards.

Overview

The aytm community refers to the people who participate in research on the platform. It includes proprietary communities, like those connected to PaidViewpoint, along with vetted panel partners. The goal is to provide reliable, diverse respondents for a wide range of projects while maintaining consistent quality controls and screening standards.

Key points

  • Combines proprietary communities and panel partners under aytm’s quality framework.
  • Supports a wide variety of targeting needs across categories and geographies.
  • Is managed through the aytm sample engine, which handles routing and quotas.

FAQs

What is the aytm community?

It is the collective term for respondents who take part in studies on aytm, including proprietary communities and panel partners managed under shared standards.

How does the community affect my sampling?

The community provides the pool of potential respondents that the sample engine draws from based on your targeting and screening criteria.

Is the aytm community global?

aytm can access respondents in multiple markets through its own communities and partner network; exact coverage depends on the audience and project needs.
