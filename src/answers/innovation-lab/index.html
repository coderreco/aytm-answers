TL;DR: The aytm innovation lab explores new research methods, ai applications, and features before they are rolled into the broader platform.

Overview

The innovation lab at aytm is focused on experimenting with new ways to collect, analyze, and apply insights. This can include advanced methodologies, novel uses of ai, or prototype features that are tested with select customers. Successful experiments inform future platform releases and help keep the research experience current with evolving technology and practices.

Key points

  • Dedicated space for testing new approaches to research and ai in practice.
  • Often works with early adopters and select customers on pilots or betas.
  • Outputs inform the roadmap for the main aytm platform and solutions.

FAQs

What is the purpose of the innovation lab?

Its purpose is to explore new methodologies and technologies in a focused way so that only proven, valuable ideas are brought into the main platform.

Can customers participate in innovation lab projects?

In some cases, customers may be invited to join pilots or beta programs; details are typically shared through account teams or announcements.

How does the lab affect the roadmap?

Learnings from the innovation lab help shape future product and ai investments, giving aytm data on what works in real-world research settings.
