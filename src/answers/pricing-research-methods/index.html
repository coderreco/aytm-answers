FAQs

What pricing methods are available on aytm?

aytm supports approaches like monadic price testing, price ladders, and guided solutions that help you evaluate different price points and understand consumer willingness to pay.

When should I use a monadic price test?

Monadic designs are useful when you want each respondent to evaluate a single price condition, reducing comparison bias and giving you cleaner reads on price acceptance or interest.

Does aytm help avoid common pricing research pitfalls?

Yes. aytm resources and xpert solutions highlight common challenges, such as unrealistic price framing or mixing too many decisions in a single question, and offer guided structures to mitigate them.
