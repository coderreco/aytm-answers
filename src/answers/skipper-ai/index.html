skipper ai on aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: skipper ai is aytm’s built-in ai assistant that helps teams design studies, interpret results, and turn findings into clear stories and deliverables.

Overview

skipper ai is embedded in the aytm platform to support key parts of the research workflow. It can help translate business questions into survey structures, surface notable patterns in the data, and summarize findings in language that stakeholders can act on. skipper ai is designed to work with your existing projects and to respect aytm’s data security and ai privacy principles.

Key points

  • Helps turn business questions into survey designs and analytic plans.
  • Assists with summarizing and framing insights for different audiences.
  • Operates within aytm’s approach to responsible ai and data privacy.

FAQs

What can skipper ai help me do?

skipper ai can assist with drafting surveys, highlighting important patterns in results, and turning data into summaries, narratives, or starter deliverables for stakeholders.

Does skipper ai replace researchers?

It is designed to augment—not replace—research and insights teams by handling repetitive tasks and helping them focus on judgment, framing, and decision-making.

How does skipper ai handle data privacy?

skipper ai is used within aytm’s broader security and ai privacy principles, which define how data is handled, where it is processed, and how client controls are respected.
