Insights and reporting

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: aytm offers interactive dashboards, crosstabs, exports, and AI-assisted summaries to help teams quickly interpret results and share clear stories with stakeholders.

Overview

As data is collected on aytm, results populate in real time. You can explore charts, apply filters, build crosstabs, and download data or visualizations. AI-powered features like skipper explore help translate statistical outcomes into plain-language insights and highlight meaningful differences across segments, saving time on manual analysis.

Key points

  • Real-time dashboards let you monitor completes and view top-line results as fieldwork progresses.
  • Crosstabs, filters, and exports support deeper analysis and integration with your own tools.
  • AI-assisted summaries help surface key patterns and turn data into narrative-ready insights.

FAQs

How do I analyze survey results in aytm?

You can use the results page to view charts, apply filters, build crosstabs, and export data or visual assets. For many studies, this built-in environment is enough to move from raw responses to clear, actionable findings.

Can I export data for further analysis?

Yes. You can export raw data, summary tables, and visualizations to work in other tools or share with stakeholders who prefer spreadsheets or slide formats.

What does skipper explore do?

skipper explore is an AI-powered analysis helper that reads your results, runs built-in statistical checks, and expresses key differences and patterns in concise, human-readable statements.
