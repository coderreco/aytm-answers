FAQs

Who are aytm resources for?

They are designed for insights professionals, marketers, product leaders, and anyone interested in how modern research and ai can support decision-making.

Do I need to be a customer to use the resources?

Many resources are publicly available, so you can learn about approaches and use cases even before you start working directly with aytm.

How often are new resources added?

New content is added periodically as aytm releases features, explores new methods, or publishes customer stories and thought leadership.
