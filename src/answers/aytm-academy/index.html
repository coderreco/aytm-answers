aytm academy overview

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: aytm academy is an online learning environment with courses, paths, and best practices for using the platform and applying research methods effectively.

Overview

aytm academy hosts self-paced content that helps teams learn how to design studies, use ai features, interpret results, and apply findings. It includes paths focused on ai at aytm, best practices for conversation ai, and other topics relevant to running research. The goal is to shorten the learning curve and create a common baseline of knowledge across teams.

Key points

  • Provides structured lessons and paths on platform features and methods.
  • Includes content on ai, survey design, analysis, and reporting.
  • Intended to support onboarding and ongoing skill-building for users.

FAQs

Who should use aytm academy?

It is designed for new and existing users, including researchers, marketers, and product team members who want to deepen their understanding of the platform and methods.

Is aytm academy included with my account?

Access details can vary by customer; your aytm representative can confirm how academy access is set up for your organization.

What kinds of topics are covered?

Topics include survey design, using ai features, interpreting data, and applying insights to decisions across product, brand, and strategy work.
