How audience impacts price and speed

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: On aytm, more niche or complex targeting typically increases cost and may slow completion, while broader, higher-incidence audiences are faster and more economical.

Overview

The audience you target has a major impact on both pricing and delivery timelines for aytm projects. Factors like incidence rate, screening criteria, geography, and quotas determine how many people need to be contacted to achieve your completes. The sample complexity meter in the platform reflects these inputs, updating cost and estimated completion time as you adjust your audience settings.

Key points

  • Low-incidence or highly niche audiences generally cost more and take longer to complete than broad, high-incidence populations.
  • Adding multiple tight quotas, layers of screening, or narrow geographies increases sample complexity.
  • The sample complexity meter shows how these factors affect price and estimated delivery before you launch.

FAQs

What is incidence rate and why does it matter?

Incidence rate is the percentage of panelists who qualify for your survey out of all attempts. Lower incidence means more invitations are needed to reach your target completes, which can increase cost and impact timing.

How does targeting complexity affect completion time?

Complex targeting—such as narrow demographic or behavioral filters, multiple quotas, or strict screeners—reduces the pool of eligible respondents. This can lengthen fieldwork compared to simpler, broader targeting.

Where can I see how my audience choices affect price and speed?

The sample complexity meter in the aytm interface shows expected cost and delivery timing for your selected audience, incidence assumptions, and completes. It updates in real time as you adjust your settings.
