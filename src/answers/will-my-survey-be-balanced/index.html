Will my survey be balanced on aytm?

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: aytm lets you use quotas, screening, and xpert solutions to balance your survey across key groups, so you meet your targeting goals and analysis needs.

Overview

On aytm, survey balance is managed through audience targeting, quotas, and routing logic. You can define who you want to reach and set quota cells to control how many completes you get in each group. For more complex studies, xpert solutions provide guided designs that bake in best practices for sample balancing and allocation.

Key points

  • Quotas allow you to control how many respondents you collect in each segment (such as age, gender, or usage level).
  • Screeners and logic ensure only qualified respondents complete the survey.
  • xpert solutions include pre-built designs with recommended balancing approaches for specific methodologies.

FAQs

How do quotas help balance my survey?

Quotas let you specify how many completes you want from different groups. The platform fills each quota cell until it reaches your targets, helping you maintain a balanced mix for analysis.

Can aytm prevent over-representation of certain groups?

Yes. When quotas are set, aytm stops recruiting respondents in a segment once the quota is filled, preventing that group from dominating the sample.

What if I am not sure how to structure my quotas?

You can use xpert solutions as a starting point or work with aytm’s team to design an appropriate sample and quota structure for your objectives.
