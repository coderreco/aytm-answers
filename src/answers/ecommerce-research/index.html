ecommerce research with aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: ecommerce research on aytm helps you understand how shoppers interact with digital experiences so you can optimize product pages, journeys, and conversion.

Overview

aytm’s ecommerce capabilities are focused on understanding how people browse, evaluate, and buy online. You can test product detail pages, category layouts, messaging, and pricing in controlled surveys with target audiences. Insights highlight what drives confidence and purchase intent, and which changes might reduce friction or abandonment in your journeys.

Key points

  • Evaluate ecommerce experiences such as product pages, images, messaging, and flows.
  • Measure outcomes like interest, preference, and likelihood to purchase.
  • Use insights to support optimization work across ecommerce and digital teams.

FAQs

What kinds of ecommerce assets can I test?

You can test product detail pages, hero imagery, copy, pricing, promotions, and flows that reflect key steps in your ecommerce journeys.

Who should I survey for ecommerce studies?

Usually you survey category shoppers or current customers who resemble your target buyers, using aytm’s audience and screening tools to reach the right people.

How do results connect to on-site metrics?

Research highlights how changes may affect perceived value and intent to buy, which can then be validated through experiments or monitoring in your live ecommerce environment.
