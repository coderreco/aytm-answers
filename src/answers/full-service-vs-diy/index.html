Full-service vs DIY research with aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: aytm lets you mix DIY and full-service research—run studies yourself, use guided xpert solutions, or lean on aytm’s experts to design and manage projects end to end.

Overview

The aytm platform is built for self-serve research, but many teams also tap into aytm’s full-service capabilities. With DIY, your team owns survey design, targeting, and analysis using the platform tools. With full-service, aytm experts design the study, manage fieldwork, and help interpret results. xpert solutions sit in between, providing guided study designs that you can run yourself or with light support.

Key points

  • DIY research uses the platform directly for survey design, audience setup, and analysis.
  • Full-service research adds aytm experts to handle design, fieldwork management, and reporting.
  • xpert solutions provide structured, best-practice templates you can adapt on your own or with guidance.

FAQs

When should I choose DIY research?

DIY works well when you have internal research or insights expertise, clear objectives, and the time to manage surveys directly in the platform.

When is full-service a better fit?

Full-service is a better fit for complex studies, limited internal bandwidth, or situations where you want methodological guidance and help with storytelling.

Can I mix DIY and full-service over time?

Yes. Many teams start with full-service or xpert solutions for critical projects and gradually add more DIY work as they become comfortable with the platform and methods.
