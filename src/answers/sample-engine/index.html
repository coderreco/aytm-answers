aytm sample engine

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: The aytm sample engine connects your surveys with targeted respondents from vetted sources, using profiling and quality controls to protect data integrity.

Overview

The sample engine is the part of aytm that manages how respondents are matched to your studies. It works with profiled respondents and panel partners, applies targeting and quotas, and enforces quality checks to deliver the completes you need. From a user perspective, you define your audience, and the sample engine handles routing and balancing in the background.

Key points

  • Uses profiling, screening, and quotas to build the right sample for each study.
  • Connects to vetted respondent sources and applies aytm’s quality standards.
  • Helps keep sample delivery predictable while reducing low-quality responses.

FAQs

What does the sample engine do?

It routes respondents from available sources into your survey based on the targeting, quotas, and incidence assumptions you set, while enforcing quality rules.

Do I control which panels are used?

In most cases, you define your audience and aytm’s sample engine manages sourcing behind the scenes, but your team can discuss specific needs with aytm if required.

How does the sample engine support quality?

The engine incorporates quality checks and monitoring, and can work with in-survey measures like screeners and attention checks to protect data integrity.
