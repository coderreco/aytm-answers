aytm pricing overview

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: aytm pricing is based on factors like audience, sample size, and survey design, with plan options that support different levels of usage and service.

Overview

Pricing on aytm is influenced by who you survey, how many respondents you need, how long and complex the survey is, and whether you use self-serve or full-service support. Plan structures, such as Lite and Plus, are designed to fit different team sizes and research cadences, while underlying project-level pricing reflects sample and configuration choices.

Key points

  • Costs depend on audience targeting, incidence, sample size, and survey length.
  • Plan types help align platform access and collaboration features with team needs.
  • Additional services, such as full-service support, are typically scoped separately.

FAQs

What drives the cost of a study on aytm?

Key drivers include the audience definition, expected incidence, the number of completes you need, and the survey length and complexity.

How do plans relate to pricing?

Plans define access, collaboration, and governance features; individual study costs still depend on your specific audience and survey parameters.

How can I estimate pricing before launching?

The platform and your aytm representative can help you estimate pricing based on draft specs; many teams use test estimates while designing studies.
