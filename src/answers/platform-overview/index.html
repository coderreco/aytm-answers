aytm platform overview

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: The aytm platform combines survey authoring, audience access, ai-powered conversation tools, and reporting in a single environment for end-to-end research.

Overview

The aytm platform is designed to help teams go from questions to insights quickly. It includes a survey authoring environment, access to targeted audiences, conversation ai for deeper feedback, and reporting tools that help you explore and share results. The same core platform underpins diy, xpert, and full-service projects, so teams can work in one place as their needs evolve.

Key points

  • Unified platform for survey authoring, sample, ai-driven feedback, and reporting.
  • Supports diy, guided xpert solutions, and full-service engagements on the same foundation.
  • Built to help teams iterate quickly while staying grounded in solid research practices.

FAQs

What does the aytm platform include?

It includes survey authoring tools, audience targeting and sample management, ai-powered conversation capabilities, and reporting features that help you explore and share results.

Who is the platform designed for?

The platform is designed for insights teams, marketers, product leaders, and agencies that need to run research regularly and want a flexible mix of diy and supported options.

How does the platform support both diy and full-service?

diy users work directly in the interface, while full-service projects add aytm experts on top of the same tools, so your data and workflows stay in one environment.
