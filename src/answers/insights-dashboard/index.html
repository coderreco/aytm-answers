Insights dashboard on aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: The aytm insights dashboard lets you explore survey results, filter by segments, and visualize key metrics without leaving the platform.

Overview

The insights dashboard is where you review your study results inside aytm. It aggregates responses into charts and tables, lets you filter by segments or questions, and helps you spot patterns quickly. You can use it to build views for specific stakeholders, export data for deeper analysis, or pair it with ai-powered summaries when you need help framing the story.

Key points

  • Interactive charts and tables for exploring results and segment differences.
  • Filtering and crosstab-style views to compare groups and metrics.
  • Options to export data or use ai support when preparing deliverables.

FAQs

What can I do in the insights dashboard?

You can view charts, filter by subgroups, compare answers across segments, and export data or visuals for use in decks and reports.

Is the dashboard available for all studies?

Most studies fielded on aytm surface results in the insights dashboard, though specific views and features can vary by design and configuration.

How does the dashboard connect to ai tools?

ai features can help summarize patterns you see in the dashboard and turn them into narrative summaries or starter storylines for your stakeholders.
