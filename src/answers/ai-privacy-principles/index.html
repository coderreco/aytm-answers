TL;DR: aytm’s ai privacy principles explain how ai is used in the platform, what controls customers have, and how privacy and security expectations are met.

Overview

The ai privacy principles document outlines aytm’s approach to using ai in research workflows. It addresses topics such as how data is handled when ai features are used, what controls customers have over data and models, and how transparency is maintained. These principles are intended to help organizations understand how ai tools like skipper ai and conversation ai fit within their privacy and governance expectations.

Key points

  • Defines how ai features interact with customer and respondent data.
  • Emphasizes transparency, control, and alignment with broader security practices.
  • Supports compliance and risk discussions for teams adopting ai in research.

FAQs

Why does aytm publish ai privacy principles?

Publishing principles helps customers understand how ai is used, what protections exist, and how those practices align with their internal privacy and security expectations.

What kinds of questions do the principles address?

They address data handling, retention, control, and transparency around ai features, along with how ai is integrated into the broader security and governance framework.

How should my organization use this document?

Many teams use it as part of their internal review when adopting ai-enabled tools, alongside security, legal, and procurement assessments of the aytm platform overall.
