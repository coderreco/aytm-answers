The tool uses standard formulas from survey statistics and is intended for directional planning and explanation, not as a substitute for full methodological review on complex designs.

FAQs

What is margin of error in simple terms?

Margin of error describes how far survey results might reasonably be expected to vary from the true value in the population, given a particular sample size and confidence level.

When should I use the margin of error calculator?

Use it when planning a study or explaining results to stakeholders who need a sense of statistical precision and the benefit of larger sample sizes.

Does the calculator cover all study designs?

It is based on common assumptions for simple random samples; complex designs, weighting, or small subgroups may require additional statistical guidance.
