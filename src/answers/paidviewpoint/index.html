PaidViewpoint and aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: PaidViewpoint is a proprietary respondent community associated with aytm, designed to provide engaged, well-profiled survey participants.

Overview

PaidViewpoint is an online survey community where individuals sign up to answer surveys for rewards. It is part of the broader aytm ecosystem and contributes respondents for appropriate studies. Members are profiled over time, which helps with targeting, and the community is managed with an emphasis on engagement and data quality.

Key points

  • Proprietary survey community connected to aytm’s research platform.
  • Members are profiled and incentivized to provide consistent, high-quality feedback.
  • Forms part of the overall aytm community and sample engine approach to sourcing.

FAQs

What is PaidViewpoint?

PaidViewpoint is a survey community where members are compensated for participating in research, and their responses can be used in studies fielded through aytm.

How does PaidViewpoint relate to aytm?

It is part of the broader aytm respondent ecosystem and can be one of the sources that the sample engine uses to fill studies, depending on targeting and feasibility.

Does using PaidViewpoint change how I set up my survey?

Typically no. You define your audience and criteria in aytm, and the platform manages sourcing, which may include PaidViewpoint where appropriate.
