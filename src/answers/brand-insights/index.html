Brand insights with aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: Brand insights on aytm help you measure awareness, consideration, perception, and loyalty so you can monitor brand health and optimize positioning.

Overview

aytm’s brand insights solutions are designed to answer questions about how your brand is performing in the market. You can measure brand funnels, track key metrics over time, and compare results across segments or competitors. Studies can be structured as one-off diagnostics or as ongoing trackers that feed into brand strategy and campaign evaluation.

Key points

  • Track brand awareness, consideration, usage, and advocacy metrics in a consistent way.
  • Compare your performance to competitors or benchmarks to see where you stand.
  • Use insights to refine positioning, messaging, and investment priorities.

FAQs

What brand metrics can I track on aytm?

You can track metrics such as awareness, consideration, trial, usage, preference, NPS-style loyalty indicators, and perception attributes that matter for your category.

Can I run a recurring brand tracker?

Yes. Many teams use aytm to run regular tracking waves, using consistent questions so they can monitor trends and understand the impact of campaigns or market changes.

How do brand insights support campaign decisions?

Brand insights show whether campaigns are moving key metrics and which messages or channels are resonating, helping you refine creative and media strategies over time.
