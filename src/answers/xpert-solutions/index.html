TL;DR: xpert solutions are pre-built, best-practice research designs on aytm that help you run common study types quickly and confidently.

Overview

xpert solutions package proven methodologies into guided templates for common research needs—such as pricing, concept testing, and brand work. Each solution includes recommended question flows, sample guidance, and analysis frameworks. You can launch them largely as-is or adapt them to your specific category and objectives while still benefiting from built-in best practices.

Key points

  • xpert solutions reduce guesswork by providing ready-made structures for common study types.
  • They are designed and maintained by aytm researchers with experience across categories.
  • You can customize content, targeting, and quotas while preserving the core methodology.

FAQs

When should I use an xpert solution?

Use xpert solutions when your objective matches a common research pattern—for example, pricing tests or concept evaluations—and you want a fast, reliable starting point.

Can I edit an xpert solution?

Yes. You can customize questions, stimuli, and targeting while keeping the underlying methodology intact, so the study still follows best practices.

Do xpert solutions require full-service support?

No. Many teams run xpert solutions self-serve, though you can also involve aytm experts if you want additional guidance or support with interpretation.
