Which aytm plan is right for me?

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: Start with lite for occasional, self-serve research; choose plus or higher tiers when you need team features, governance, and more advanced tools or services.

Overview

aytm offers different plans so individuals, small teams, and large organizations can match access and features to their needs. lite is designed for users who run research occasionally and want to explore core capabilities at low commitment. plus adds collaboration, advanced tools, and expanded limits for teams running recurring programs. Larger organizations often work with aytm on custom or enterprise-style agreements for governance, services, and scale.

Key points

  • lite is best for individuals or small teams with irregular survey needs.
  • plus is suited to teams running frequent or ongoing research that need collaboration and more advanced capabilities.
  • Enterprise-style arrangements are available for organizations that need governance, support, and larger-scale programs.

FAQs

When should I choose lite?

Choose lite if you are testing the platform, are a smaller team, or only run research occasionally. It lets you access core tools without committing to a larger program on day one.

When should I move to plus?

Move to plus when research becomes a recurring part of your workflow, you have multiple users, or you need more advanced tools, collaboration features, or program-level support.

How do I evaluate which plan is right for my organization?

Consider your research volume, number of users, governance requirements, and desired level of service. If you are unsure, you can start with lite and speak with the aytm team to evaluate whether plus or an enterprise option is a better long-term fit.
