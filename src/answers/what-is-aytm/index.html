What is aytm?

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: aytm is an agile consumer insights platform that lets teams design surveys, reach targeted audiences, and analyze results in one integrated environment.

Overview

aytm (Ask Your Target Market) is a fully integrated market research platform that connects you with verified audiences and automates survey tests from design through analysis. It combines a predictive sample engine, intuitive survey authoring, and powerful insights tools to help teams tap into consumer voice earlier and more often.

Key points

  • aytm connects you with verified consumer audiences and automates research tests from setup to results.
  • The platform supports a wide range of studies, including concept tests, brand research, pricing work, and customer experience projects.
  • Teams can work self-serve or pair the platform with expert services for more complex initiatives.

FAQs

What does aytm do?

aytm provides technology and services to run survey-based market research. You can design questionnaires, target audiences, launch fieldwork, and analyze results in one system instead of working with separate tools or vendors.

Who is aytm for?

aytm is built for insights professionals, marketers, product teams, and agencies that need fast, reliable consumer data. It supports everything from quick tests to ongoing research programs.

How is aytm different from a traditional panel provider?

aytm combines panel access with a full research platform. Instead of only buying sample, you also get survey authoring, automation, and analysis tools tailored for agile, iterative insights work.
