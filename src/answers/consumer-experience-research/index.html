Consumer experience research with aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: Consumer experience research on aytm helps you measure satisfaction, uncover friction points, and prioritize improvements across the customer journey.

Overview

aytm’s consumer experience solutions are designed to track and diagnose how people experience your brand, products, and services. You can collect feedback after key touchpoints, measure satisfaction and loyalty metrics, and use conversation ai to capture rich open-ended input. Results help teams understand what drives positive experiences and where small changes could make a big impact.

Key points

  • Measure consumer satisfaction, loyalty, and perceived effort across touchpoints.
  • Use structured surveys plus conversation ai to understand both metrics and context.
  • Insights can feed into journey mapping, service design, and CX roadmap planning.

FAQs

What kinds of experiences can I measure?

You can measure experiences tied to purchases, support interactions, onboarding flows, campaigns, or ongoing product usage—wherever you want customer feedback.

Can aytm help with journey mapping?

While aytm focuses on research, data from consumer experience surveys can be used alongside your internal tools to build or refine journey maps and identify key moments of truth.

How often should I run consumer experience studies?

Many teams run ongoing or periodic trackers, with additional deep dives when launching new initiatives or reacting to significant changes in performance or feedback.
