Full-service research with aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: Full-service research on aytm pairs the platform with a team of experts who design, manage, and help interpret studies for you.

Overview

While aytm is built for self-serve research, many organizations choose full-service support for complex or high-stakes initiatives. In a full-service engagement, aytm researchers work with you to clarify objectives, design the study, manage programming and fieldwork, and support analysis and storytelling. This model is especially useful when internal bandwidth is limited or specialized methodological expertise is needed.

Key points

  • Full-service engagements cover design, fieldwork management, and support with reporting or storytelling.
  • aytm experts bring category and methodological experience to complex studies.
  • You can mix full-service projects with DIY work on the same platform over time.

FAQs

When should I choose full-service research?

Full-service is a good fit for complex, multi-country, or high-visibility studies, or when your internal team lacks the time or specialized expertise to manage the project themselves.

What does the aytm team handle in full-service projects?

The team typically handles survey design, programming, sample planning, field management, and can also assist with analysis, reporting, and recommendations.

Can full-service and DIY coexist?

Yes. Many organizations run a mix of full-service and DIY projects, using full-service support for critical initiatives and DIY for day-to-day questions and agile work.
