conversation ai on aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: conversation ai is an ai-driven, dialog-style question type on aytm that encourages respondents to share deeper, more natural feedback at scale.

Overview

conversation ai replaces static open-ended questions with a guided, conversational interaction. Respondents answer in their own words while the system probes and follows up, leading to richer detail without requiring a human moderator. For researchers, the output is structured so it can be explored alongside quant metrics and turned into themes and stories more quickly.

Key points

  • Uses ai to guide follow-up prompts and deepen responses in real time.
  • Generates richer qualitative-style feedback within a quantitative survey.
  • Is integrated with aytm reporting and lives within its security and ai privacy framework.

FAQs

How is conversation ai different from a traditional open-end?

Traditional open-ends collect a single response, while conversation ai can ask follow-up questions in the same interaction, encouraging respondents to elaborate and clarify their thoughts.

Where does conversation ai fit in my survey?

It is often used at key moments—for example after concept exposure, experience ratings, or brand perceptions—where understanding the “why” adds important context.

How is the output analyzed?

Responses can be reviewed as transcripts, grouped into themes, and linked to quant variables in the aytm reporting environment, helping you move from raw text to insights more quickly.
