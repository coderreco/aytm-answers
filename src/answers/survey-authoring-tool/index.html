Survey authoring on aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: The aytm survey authoring tool gives you flexible question types, logic, quotas, and ai-powered options to design studies that fit your objectives.

Overview

aytm’s survey authoring environment is where you translate business questions into structured research. You can choose from multiple question types, apply skip logic and branching, set quotas, and embed conversation ai where you need deeper feedback. Authoring tools are designed for speed and clarity, so you can iterate quickly while keeping the respondent experience streamlined.

Key points

  • Supports a wide range of question types, from simple scales to advanced constructs.
  • Includes logic, branching, and quotas to control respondent experience and sample balance.
  • Integrates with ai features like conversation ai and skipper ai for added depth and guidance.

FAQs

Who can use the survey authoring tool?

It is designed for researchers and non-researchers, with features that support both simple, quick-turn work and more complex, methodologically rigorous designs.

Can I reuse or adapt past surveys?

Yes. You can duplicate existing surveys, edit them for new objectives, and build a library of templates that your team can reuse over time.

How does ai support survey authoring?

ai tools like skipper ai can help you draft questions, suggest structures, and refine wording, while conversation ai provides richer ways to capture open-ended feedback within your surveys.
