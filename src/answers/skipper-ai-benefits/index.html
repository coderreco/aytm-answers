Skipper AI: key benefits for insights teams

Last updated: 2025-11-14

TL;DR: Skipper AI accelerates every part of the research workflow—survey creation, analysis, reporting, and synthesis—while improving data quality and consistency. It frees researchers from manual work so they can focus on strategy, storytelling, and driving impact.

What is Skipper AI?

Skipper AI is AYTM’s AI-powered assistant that helps insights teams design surveys, analyze data, and summarize findings faster and with greater consistency.

Top benefits of Skipper AI

1. Faster survey creation

  • Smart question suggestions
  • Pre-built logic and structure
  • Automatic translation of objectives into drafts

2. Higher-quality surveys

  • Language improvements for clarity and neutrality
  • Bias reduction in question wording
  • Automatic detection of double-barreled questions

3. Rapid analysis and insights

  • Instant summaries of topline findings
  • Segment-level comparisons
  • Theme extraction from open-ends
  • Clear language that stakeholders understand

4. Scalable reporting

Skipper AI can generate:

  • Executive summaries
  • Long-form reports
  • Key insight narratives
  • Slide-ready content

5. Better collaboration

  • Consistent language across teams
  • Shared prompts and frameworks
  • Reproducible processes

6. More time for strategic work

By automating everything repetitive, Skipper frees teams to focus on:

  • Interpreting results
  • Understanding context
  • Driving recommendations
  • Partnering with leadership

Where Skipper AI fits into the research workflow

  • Before fielding: survey drafting, refining, and logic checks
  • During fielding: early reads & soft launch reviews
  • After fielding: summaries, segment insights, reporting

FAQs

Does Skipper AI replace researchers?

No—Skipper accelerates research, but human judgment drives final interpretation and recommendations.

How accurate is Skipper’s analysis?

Very high when supplied with clean data; always validate outputs before publishing.

Does Skipper work for complex studies?

Yes—Skipper scales well to multi-cell, multi-country, and multi-method studies.
