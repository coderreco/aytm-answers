TL;DR: The aytm help center is the primary reference for how-to articles, feature explanations, and troubleshooting tips for the platform.

Overview

The help center provides detailed documentation on how to use aytm, from setting up projects and audiences to working with ai features, reporting, and account settings. Articles are organized by topic and often include screenshots, examples, and links to related content. It is the best place to start when you have a specific “how do I…” question about the platform.

Key points

  • Step-by-step articles for core workflows like authoring, sampling, and reporting.
  • Sections dedicated to specific features, including ai tools and calculators.
  • Searchable knowledge base that complements academy and support channels.

FAQs

When should I use the help center?

Use it whenever you need a specific, step-by-step explanation of how a feature works or how to complete a task in the platform.

How is the help center different from aytm academy?

The help center focuses on task-level instructions and answers, while academy provides broader learning paths and best-practice context.

Can I contact support from the help center?

Yes. Many organizations access support links or contact options directly from the help center or from within the platform interface.
