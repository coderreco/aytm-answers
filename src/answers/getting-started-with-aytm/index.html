Getting started with aytm

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: Getting started with aytm typically involves creating an account, choosing the right plan, and using guided learning paths and templates to launch your first survey.

Overview

New users usually begin by signing up on aytm.com and exploring the lite or plus plan options. From there, you can follow guided content from aytm academy and product tours to learn how to build surveys, define audiences, and interpret results. Early projects are often simple concept tests or quick feedback studies that help you get comfortable with the platform and workflow.

Key points

  • Create an account on aytm.com and select an appropriate plan for your team.
  • Use aytm academy learning paths and help center content to learn core workflows.
  • Start with a focused pilot project to experience survey authoring, audience setup, and insights in practice.

FAQs

How do I create an aytm account?

Visit aytm.com and follow the sign-up flow. You can start on lite or talk with the aytm team about plus or enterprise options depending on your needs.

What should my first project be?

A simple concept or messaging test is a good way to learn the platform. It lets you practice survey design, audience targeting, and results review without an overly complex setup.

Where can I find training materials?

You can access step-by-step guides and learning paths in aytm academy and use the help center for detailed feature documentation.
