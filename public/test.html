About aytm (company overview)

Last updated: 2025-11-13

TL;DR: aytm is an automated market research platform that helps teams design surveys, reach targeted audiences, and analyze results in one integrated environment.

Overview

aytm (Ask Your Target Market) is a consumer insights platform used by insights, marketing, and product teams to run survey-based research. It brings together survey authoring, panel sampling, and reporting so teams can move quickly from business questions to data-driven answers. Organizations use aytm to test concepts, ads, products, and customer experiences with precisely targeted respondents, while maintaining control over methodology and data quality.

Key points

  • aytm combines survey authoring, sample, and analysis in a single platform for agile research workflows.
  • The platform supports a wide range of use cases, including product development, brand and campaign measurement, and customer or user experience research.
  • Teams can work self-serve or add expert support and services for more complex or strategic projects.

FAQs

What is aytm?

aytm is a market research platform that lets teams design surveys, recruit respondents, and analyze results all in one place. It is built for agile, iterative research, so you can quickly test ideas, messages, products, and experiences with your target audience.

What is aytm used for?

Teams use aytm for concept and product testing, brand and campaign measurement, pricing and packaging work, customer and user experience research, and broader strategic studies such as market landscape or segmentation projects.

Who typically uses aytm?

aytm is used by insights professionals, marketers, product managers, and research agencies who need fast, reliable consumer data. It supports both individual practitioners running quick projects and larger organizations operating recurring research programs.
